Cache invalidation remains one of the hardest problems in computer science, but modern frameworks provide structured approaches. Time-based expiration, tag-based purging, and stale-while-revalidate patterns each offer different tradeoffs between freshness and performance. Choosing the right strategy depends on how frequently your data changes and how tolerant your users are of stale content.
